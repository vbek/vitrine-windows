VITRINE FOR WINDOWS
Digital signage that runs on this PC. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

START
1. Double-click "Start Vitrine".
2. Your browser opens. Create the owner account on this PC.
3. Keep the black window open while you use Vitrine. Closing it stops Vitrine.

PHONES AND OTHER SCREENS
The black window shows an address such as http://192.168.1.20:3400.
Open it on a device on the same Wi-Fi to manage content. Add /tv to the end to use the device as a screen.
This PC must be on, with Vitrine running. If Windows asks, allow Vitrine on private networks.
Traffic on your network isn't encrypted, so only use Vitrine on networks you trust.

YOUR DATA
Your database and uploads are in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Vitrine\data on this PC. Nothing is sent to the website you downloaded this from.
Vitrine contacts other services only for content you add, such as web pages, YouTube, news feeds and weather.

UPDATE
Close Vitrine, delete this folder, and extract the new version. Your data is kept.

UNINSTALL
Close Vitrine and delete this folder. To delete your data as well, delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\Vitrine.
